About Me

Passionate about building impactful digital solutions through software, AI, and product thinking.

Hello! I'm Jidnyesha Sankhe, an Electronics & Computer Science Engineer with a strong interest in Software Development, Artificial Intelligence, and Project Management. I enjoy transforming ideas into practical, user-focused digital products that solve real-world problems.

My technical experience includes developing modern web applications using React, Next.js, Node.js, Django, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and Supabase. I also have hands-on experience with REST APIs, Git, Agile methodologies, and software development life cycle (SDLC) practices.

Working with diverse teams has improved my communication, planning, and project execution abilities while helping me understand the importance of delivering solutions that create meaningful impact.

I am actively seeking opportunities in Software Development, Full Stack Development, Product Management, and Technical Project Management, where I can contribute, learn continuously, and grow alongside innovative teams.
